WarpDrop sends files straight from one device to another, end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud, there is no account, and the files never sit on a server.

<br><b>Features:</b>

- Nearby devices on your Wi-Fi show up in the Send tab: tap one and send. Works with no internet at all
- Share with a short code or a QR code: the receiver can use this app, the web app or the command line
- Direct device-to-device transfer (WebRTC), with an encrypted relay as the fallback when a direct path is blocked
- Share → WarpDrop from any app
- Large files and many files, written to your Downloads as they arrive and checked with a checksum before they are published
- Material 3 Expressive design with Material You colors and an AMOLED theme
- Self-hostable: point the app at your own server in Settings
- Open source, no ads, no tracking

Works with the WarpDrop web app (drop.warplabs.info) and the warpdrop command-line tool.
